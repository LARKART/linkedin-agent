## 5. Brand Personality

### The brand should feel

**Technical** --- understands systems deeply.\
**Practical** --- focuses on what can actually be implemented.\
**Human** --- communicates like a real person, not a corporate
brochure.\
**Curious** --- explores new AI capabilities and experiments with
emerging tools.\
**Reliable** --- emphasizes structure, validation, error handling, and
maintainability.\
**Long-term** --- positions the relationship as ongoing support rather
than a one-off build.

### Avoid

-   Overhyped AI language
-   Generic "AI will change everything" messaging
-   Excessive jargon
-   Fake urgency
-   Aggressive sales language
-   Empty claims such as "10x your business"
-   Making every solution sound fully autonomous when human approval is
    useful

## 6. Voice & Tone

### Default voice

Clear, conversational, technical, confident, and approachable.

Write like a knowledgeable technical partner explaining something to a
business owner.

### Preferred style

-   Short paragraphs
-   Strong first sentence
-   Concrete examples
-   Explain the business problem before the technology
-   Use plain English
-   Show the workflow or outcome
-   Use technical terms when they add precision
-   End with a natural question or invitation when appropriate

### Example

Instead of:

> Our revolutionary AI automation ecosystem leverages cutting-edge
> technologies to transform business operations.

Use:

> Most teams don't need another AI tool. They need the tools they
> already use to work together. I build the workflows that connect them.

## 8. LinkedIn Content Framework

### Recommended post structure

**Hook**\
Start with a specific observation, problem, result, or lesson.

**Context**\
Explain what was happening before the system.

**Build**\
Show how the workflow works.

**Business impact**\
Explain what changed: time, consistency, visibility, response speed,
manual effort, or process reliability.

**Lesson**\
Share one useful insight.

**Conversation CTA**\
Invite discussion without forcing a sales pitch.

### Example framework

> Most businesses don't have an AI problem.\
> They have a workflow problem.
>
> I recently built a system that connects \[tools/process\].
>
> Instead of: - copying data - rewriting content - checking multiple
> tools - manually updating records
>
> the workflow handles the repetitive steps automatically.
>
> The interesting part isn't the AI model.
>
> It's the orchestration around it.
>
> That's where AI becomes useful in a real business.

`#50C878`

Use for: - Brand accents - Background shapes - Buttons - Highlights -
Supporting graphics

**Orange**\
`#FFA500`

Use for: - Calls to action - Key words - Attention accents - Energy and
emphasis

**White**\
`#FFFFFF`

Use for: - Clean backgrounds - Negative space - Text on dark areas -
High-contrast layouts

### Supporting visual direction

-   Dark navy / deep green can be used for contrast
-   Keep layouts clean and spacious
-   Use rounded shapes and organic curves
-   Use orange sparingly as an attention color
-   Keep the brand modern rather than overly futuristic
-   Avoid excessive gradients and visual clutter

### Logo direction

The LARK identity shown in the banner uses a strong wordmark inside a
circular/spherical visual.

When creating new assets: - Keep the LARK name prominent - Preserve
strong geometric simplicity - Maintain emerald + orange + white as the
recognizable palette - Avoid changing the logo into unrelated visual
styles

## 12. Offer Architecture

Position services around outcomes rather than tool names.

### Offer A --- Automation Audit

Identify: - Repetitive tasks - Manual handoffs - Disconnected systems -
Lead leakage - Data duplication - Follow-up gaps

Deliverable: A practical automation roadmap.

### Offer B --- AI Workflow Build

Build one focused workflow from start to finish.

Examples: - Lead capture → qualification → CRM - Gmail → contact
database - Content research → AI repurposing → approval → publishing -
Form → AI processing → database → notification

### Offer C --- AI Agent System

Design an agent with: - Defined role - Instructions - Tools - Memory or
knowledge - Guardrails - Human approval where required - Monitoring and
error handling

### Offer D --- Long-Term AI Automation Partner

Ongoing: - Workflow improvements - New integrations - AI agent
development - Monitoring - Optimization - New automation opportunities

This supports the agency promise of long-term business support.

## 16. Brand Guardrails

### Always

-   Be specific
-   Demonstrate the workflow
-   Connect technology to business outcomes
-   Explain what was actually built
-   Be transparent about limitations
-   Use real examples when available
-   Keep the tone human
-   Build trust before selling

### Never

-   Pretend a prototype is production-ready
-   Claim guaranteed business results
-   Use fake statistics
-   Overpromise autonomy
-   Spam prospects
-   Make every post a sales pitch
-   Use technical complexity as a substitute for value

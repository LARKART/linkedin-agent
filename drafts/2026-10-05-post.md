**Topic:** AI agents entering commerce and retail workflows
**Why now:** Multiple fresh reports within the last 2 hours point to agents being forecast to drive significant online sales and already being deployed in retail operations, suggesting a near-term shift in how GTM and sales workflows are structured

## Caption

Analysts are now forecasting AI agents will directly influence online retail sales, not just assist with support tickets.

That's a signal worth paying attention to, separate from whether the specific number holds up.

Most GTM teams still route leads the same way: form fills land in a CRM, someone reviews them, someone else follows up. An agent sitting in that path doesn't need hype. It needs the qualification logic a human rep already uses, written down clearly enough to automate.

That's the actual gap I see in most sales workflows. Not a lack of AI tools, but a lack of documented decision logic for what counts as a qualified lead, what triggers follow-up, and what needs a person.

Agents can only be as good as the workflow they're dropped into. CRM automation, lead scoring rules, and clean handoffs between tools matter more right now than which agent framework you pick.

If your qualification process only lives in someone's head, that's the place to start before adding an agent on top of it.

What does your lead qualification process actually look like today? Written down, or just known by the team?

## Caption

Recent conversations about AI agent token costs are hitting a nerve. Many businesses are realizing that as these systems get more complex, the spend isn't always what they expected. 

It's easy for token usage to escalate, especially with multi-step agentic workflows that involve multiple tool calls, extensive reasoning, or RAG lookups. Each interaction, each re-prompt, each API call adds to the bill.

The real challenge isn't just knowing what you spend, but understanding if that spend delivers a clear return. Are these advanced AI systems actually reducing manual work, improving follow-up, or connecting your tools in a way that justifies the cost?

Before you scale your agentic AI, ask these questions: 

1. Where are the highest token costs coming from?
2. Can we optimize the prompts or workflow steps?
3. What's the measurable business outcome for this specific agent?

## Caption

Many think AI agents are for complex, 'moonshot' projects.

But the most effective AI systems start small.

Identify one specific, repetitive task.

Map its current manual steps.

Then, build a simple agent to handle just that.

This focused approach ensures it fits your team's real workflow.

It reduces friction immediately, giving you clear wins.

Scaling comes later, based on real results.

Where's one process you could simplify with a small AI agent?

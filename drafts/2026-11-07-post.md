## Caption

Everyone debugs the wrong part of their automation.

They check the AI model.
They check the prompt.
They check the integration logs.

The actual break is usually smaller than that.

It's the handoff.

The moment data moves from one tool to another and nobody decided what happens if a field is empty, a format changes, or an API times out.

I see this constantly in client workflows.

The lead scoring works fine. The CRM update works fine. But the five seconds between them has no error handling, no retry, no fallback.

So one Tuesday, a webhook times out and three days of leads just vanish from the pipeline.

Nobody notices until someone asks why the numbers look light.

A workflow isn't reliable because the AI step is smart. It's reliable because someone thought through what happens when the handoff fails.

Where does your current setup have the weakest handoff between tools?

## Caption

Most agent builds skip the one step that actually matters.

Everyone wants full autonomy.

Nobody wants to be the one explaining why the agent emailed a client the wrong invoice.

Here is what I have learned building these systems.

Autonomy is not the goal.

Control at the right moment is.

A good agent knows three things:

What it can decide alone.

What needs a second pair of eyes.

When to stop and ask.

That third one is the hard part.

Most teams design for the happy path.

Few design for the moment the agent is unsure.

That is exactly where a human checkpoint earns its keep.

Not because AI is unreliable.

Because business outcomes carry real consequences.

A missed step in a demo costs nothing.

A missed step in production costs a client.

So before you ship the next agent, ask where it should pause, not just where it should act.

Where are you still letting agents run fully unsupervised in your business right now?

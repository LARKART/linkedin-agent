## Caption

Most automations die in the handoff.

Not in the task itself.

A workflow can scrape data, write copy, send an email.

But the moment it needs to pass context to the next step, things break.

A lead comes in.
Someone qualifies it manually.
Then someone else copies it into the CRM.
Then someone else follows up late.

Every handoff is a place where information gets lost, delayed, or forgotten.

The fix isn't a smarter AI model.

It's designing the handoff itself.

What happens to the output.
Who or what receives it.
What triggers the next step.
What happens if that step fails.

I spend more time thinking about handoffs than about the AI part.

That's usually where the real work is.

Where does your process actually break down, the task or the handoff?

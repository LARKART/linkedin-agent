## Caption

Most automations don't fail from bad logic.

They fail because nobody owns them after launch.

Here's what I mean.

A workflow goes live.
It works great for three weeks.

Then a field name changes in the CRM.
Or an API updates its response format.
Or someone adds a new step to the sales process manually, without telling anyone.

The automation keeps running.
It just runs wrong.

No error message. No alert.
Just quietly bad data piling up until someone notices three months later.

The fix isn't more AI.

It's assigning a real person to watch the workflow, check the logs, and update it when the business changes.

Every system I build gets a named owner before it goes live.
Not a team. A person.

Someone whose job includes noticing when the automation and the business have drifted apart.

What's one automation in your business that nobody actually checks on anymore?

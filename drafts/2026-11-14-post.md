## Caption

Most delays in a business aren't caused by slow people.

They're caused by slow handoffs.

Someone finishes a task and the work just sits there.

Waiting for an email to get read.
Waiting for someone to check a spreadsheet.
Waiting for a manual update that could've happened instantly.

I see this constantly when I look at how teams actually operate.

The work itself usually isn't the bottleneck.

It's the gap between one step finishing and the next one starting.

That gap is exactly what automation is good at closing.

Not replacing the thinking.
Not replacing the judgment calls.

Just removing the dead time between steps so people can focus on the parts that actually need a human.

Where does work sit and wait the longest on your team right now?

## Caption

Most automations don't break loudly.

They break quietly.

A field stops updating.
A webhook silently times out.
An API key expires on a Tuesday nobody checks.

No error message. No alert. Just a workflow that looks fine and has been failing for three weeks.

I see this a lot with businesses who automate a process, feel the relief of not touching it anymore, and then stop watching it entirely.

The automation isn't the finish line.

The finish line is knowing when it stops doing its job.

That means:

Logging what happened, not just that something ran

Alerts when a step fails, not just when the whole workflow crashes

A simple way to check if data actually landed where it should

None of this is glamorous. It's also the difference between a workflow that saves time and one that quietly costs you a client.

What's one automation in your business that nobody has checked on in months?

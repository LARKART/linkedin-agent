## Caption

Most automations don't fail loudly.

They fail quietly.

A workflow breaks on a Tuesday.
Nobody notices until Friday.
By then the CRM has bad data, the follow-up never went out, and someone is asking why a lead went cold.

Here's the part people skip when they build automations.

They build the happy path.
Form fills out correctly, API responds fast, data lands where it should.

They don't build for the version where the API times out, the field is empty, or the token expired.

A workflow that runs without errors in testing is not the same as a workflow that runs reliably for six months.

The second one needs logging.
It needs alerts when something breaks.
It needs a person who gets a message that says "this step failed" instead of finding out from a client.

The automation isn't the hard part anymore.
Watching it is.

What's one automation in your business that you'd actually know about if it silently broke tomorrow?

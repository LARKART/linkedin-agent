## Caption

Most automations fail quietly.

No error message.
No alert.
Just wrong data sitting in your CRM for three weeks before anyone notices.

Here's the part people skip when they build their first workflow.

They focus on the happy path.

Form fills out correctly.
API responds the way it should.
Data lands where it's supposed to.

Then one API changes a field name.
Or a rate limit kicks in.
Or a lead fills in their email wrong.

And the workflow doesn't crash.

It just keeps running, quietly producing garbage, because nobody built in a way to catch it.

The fix isn't more automation.

It's a simple layer most people treat as optional:

Logging what happened.
Flagging what looks off.
Alerting a real person when something breaks the pattern.

That's the unglamorous 20 percent of the build.

It's also the only part that makes the other 80 percent trustworthy.

What's the last automation you built that broke without telling you?

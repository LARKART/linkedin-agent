## Caption

Most automation projects fail before the first workflow goes live.

Not because of the tools.

Because nobody mapped the process first.

I see this constantly.

A business wants to automate lead follow-up.

But nobody can explain what actually happens between a form fill and a closed deal.

Who checks the lead.

What makes it qualified.

When someone gets a reply.

Why some leads sit for three days.

If you cannot explain the process in plain English, automating it just makes the mess move faster.

The fix is boring.

Write the current process down, step by step, before touching n8n or Zapier or any agent.

Then automate the steps that are actually repeatable.

Leave judgment calls to people until you trust the pattern.

What's one process in your business you've never actually written down step by step?

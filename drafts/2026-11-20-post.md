## Caption

Nobody's workflow problem is a tools problem.

I see this every week.

A business has a CRM.
A calendar app.
An email tool.
A spreadsheet for tracking leads.

Every single tool works fine on its own.

The actual problem is the space between them.

Someone copies a lead from a form into the CRM.
Someone checks three places before a call.
Someone updates a sheet that nothing else reads.

That gap is where time disappears.

Not in the tools.
In the handoff between them.

When I build a workflow, I'm not adding another app to the stack.

I'm closing the gap between the ones already there.

Form fills in.
Data moves on its own.
The right person gets notified.
Nothing gets copied twice.

That's the whole job most of the time.

Not more software.
Less distance between the software you already pay for.

What's the gap in your workflow that nobody's closed yet?

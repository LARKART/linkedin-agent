## Caption

Everyone debugs the wrong part of their workflow.

Here's what I mean.

A workflow breaks.
First instinct: check the logic.
Rewrite the steps.
Add more conditions.

But nine times out of ten, the logic was fine.

The real problem was upstream.

A field that's sometimes empty.
A date format that changes between tools.
A lead with no email, just a phone number.
A webhook that fires twice.

Your automation isn't reasoning wrong.
It's reasoning on bad input.

So before you touch the workflow steps, look at what's actually flowing into them.

Where does the data get messy.
Where does it get duplicated.
Where does it arrive incomplete.

Clean the input and half your 'logic problems' disappear on their own.

What's the messiest data source feeding one of your workflows right now?

## Caption

Most automation projects fail before a single workflow runs.

Here's why.

Teams map out their messy process.
Then they automate the mess.

Now it just runs faster.

I see this constantly with lead handoffs.
Sales forgets to update the CRM.
Marketing sends leads with no context.
Follow-up happens whenever someone remembers.

Automate that exact flow and you get the same gaps.
Just quicker and with less visibility into where it broke.

The fix isn't more tools.

It's fixing the process first.

Write down what should happen.
Not what currently happens.

Then build the system around that.

What's one step in your process you'd actually redesign before automating it?

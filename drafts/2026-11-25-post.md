## Caption

Nobody plans for the gap between two systems.

They plan for the systems.

I see this constantly when I audit a client's workflow.

The CRM works fine.
The email tool works fine.
The form works fine.

Then someone built a manual step between them years ago and forgot about it.

A person checking a spreadsheet.
A person copying a lead into another tool.
A person approving something in a Slack message that nobody else sees.

That step becomes invisible because it works, until the person goes on holiday or changes roles.

Then the whole workflow quietly stops and nobody notices for a week.

When I design a workflow now, I spend more time on the handoffs than the tools themselves.

What happens when a field is empty.
What happens when an API times out.
What happens when two records almost match but not quite.

The tools rarely fail.

The space between them does.

Where does your workflow actually break, at the tool level or at the handoff between two people or systems?

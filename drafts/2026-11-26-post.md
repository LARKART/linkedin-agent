## Caption

Your CRM is not the problem.

The handoffs are.

I keep seeing teams add more fields, more tags, more custom properties to a CRM that's already struggling.

But the lead isn't getting lost because a field is missing.

It's getting lost between the form, the inbox, the spreadsheet, and the person who finally updates the record three days later.

Every manual handoff is a place where:

follow-up slows down

context gets dropped

someone forgets to update the next tool

One workflow I'd rather build than another dashboard:

form submission routes straight into qualification logic, qualification pushes into the CRM automatically, and the right person gets notified the moment it matters.

No copy-paste. No "I'll update that later."

The CRM isn't the system. It's just where the system ends up.

Where's the handoff in your process that still happens by hand?

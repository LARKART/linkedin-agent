## Caption

Nobody talks about the boring part of automation.

The handoff.

Everyone builds the flashy step.
The AI that writes the email.
The agent that scores the lead.
The bot that drafts the reply.

Then the output just sits there.

No one connects it to the next system.
No one decides who owns it next.
No one checks it landed where it needed to.

I see this constantly in CRM and lead workflows.

The AI step works fine.

The handoff to the human or the next tool is where it quietly breaks.

A lead gets scored but never reaches sales.
A summary gets written but never gets read.
A task gets created but never gets assigned.

Good automation design spends more time on the handoff than the task itself.

Who receives this.
What happens if they don't act.
How does the system know it actually worked.

The task is the easy 20 percent.

The handoff is the part that decides if the whole thing is trustworthy.

Where does your process usually break, at the task or at the handoff?
